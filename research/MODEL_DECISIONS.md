# Selected local stack and measured decisions

- **Translation:** Marian OPUS-MT English→Hindi ONNX int8, CPU, approximately 110 MB of encoder/decoder weights. Compared actual outputs on 32 reference lines with the approximately 107 MB Argos/OpenNMT baseline. Neither understands scene history or reliably localizes slang. Marian was chosen for correct basic commands such as “Don't move” and more conversational output on some examples. Sentence splitting fixes observed omission of neighboring sentences. Curated reference/profile overrides remain explicitly separate from model inference. Raw results are retained in `artifacts/marian-evaluation.json` and `artifacts/argos-evaluation.json`.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M v1.0; four Hindi identities, two benchmarked over ten lines. The first int8 export took 14–17 seconds for a short line and was rejected. An alternative FP16 export took roughly 1.5–1.8 seconds but produced silent/non-finite output in one case. Promoting the export's tensor/arithmetic types to FP32 fixed the observed failure across the 20-sample rerun. This is not a restoration of original full-precision weights. CPU has two inference threads and workers run BelowNormal.
- **GPU:** DirectML was tested and failed on a ConvTranspose operation. It is not used or required by the shipped stack. The final runtime explicitly selects CPUExecutionProvider and disables ONNX Runtime telemetry events. No CUDA/PyTorch installation or multi-gigabyte model was needed.
- **ASR:** Vosk small English 0.15, approximately 41 MB download. Local 16 kHz mono PCM input comes from Windows process-specific loopback. No microphone capture, source separation or acoustic emotion inference is implemented.
- **Playback:** shared-mode WASAPI with a requested 60 ms buffer. Multi-sentence Hindi overlaps synthesis/playback with one waiting sentence; cache entries are committed only for complete utterances. Metrics end at the first playback request, excluding acoustic/hardware arrival. Dialogue queue capacity is one pending line; confirmed newer dialogue cancels old generation/playback.

Hindi speech is not automatically rated professional. Kokoro's documented Hindi training coverage is limited, and this implementation has no expressive emotion controls. Chatterbox remains a future quality candidate rather than a tested integration. XTTS/Piper licensing and IndicTrans2 access restrictions are recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Official sources: [Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), [ONNX export](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX), [Marian](https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-hi), [Marian export](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/opus-mt-en-hi), [Vosk models](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models), [Microsoft process loopback](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/samples/microsoft/windows-classic-samples/applicationloopbackaudio-sample/), [DirectML](https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/execution-providers/DirectML-ExecutionProvider.html).
